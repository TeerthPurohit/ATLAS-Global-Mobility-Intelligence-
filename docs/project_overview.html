NYC TLC Ride Intelligence Platform

An end-to-end data platform over ~8–10M NYC High-Volume For-Hire Vehicle trips: ingested to DuckDB, transformed with dbt, enriched by hand-built spatial / graph / time-series algorithms, forecast by a four-model ML/DL ladder, and made conversational through a hybrid RAG chat — served via FastAPI + React.
